Demand Step B: Account for changes in collective heating demand clusters during the heating transition
======================================================================================================

The collective heating demand cluster we just defined will transform during the heating
transition.

That means we need to account for various changes we know or expect to occur in the
clusters, e.g. insulation of dwellings and buildings, demolition or (re)construction of
neighbourhoods, effects of climate change and different consumer behaviour w.r.t.
heating demand.

These changes are effectuated in the properties of the collective heating demand
cluster, e.g. the demand area for which demand is aggregated, associated heating demand
profile and costs.

And they should be pinned to a timeline the future moments we expect the changes to
occur.

For this, we use the scenario manager.
