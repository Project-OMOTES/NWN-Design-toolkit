Production Step B: Account for changes in the source mix towards 2050
=====================================================================

The source mix will change during the heating transition, as existing sources may be
outphased and new (potential) sources become part of the source mix.

These changes over time are effectuated with the scenario manager.

In this Step by step, we look into future scenarios of changing commodity prices and new
potential sources

1. Create/update the baseline ESDL


   * Example 1: Commodity prices


      * Define variants - ESDL files that reflect gas prices increasing by 50%, 100% and
        200% respectively for the years 2030, 2040 and 2050, compared to the baseline.


   * Example 1: Waste heat potential


      * Define variants - ESDL files that reflect a potential waste heat source that
        becomes available as part of the source mix by 2040
